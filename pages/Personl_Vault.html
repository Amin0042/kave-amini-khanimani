Personal Vault

Personal and self-initiated work in three collections: Traditional (ink, graphite and watercolour on paper), Digital (illustration made on screen) and Cyborg (AI-assisted experiments). Together they show one visual language moving between the hand, the screen and the machine.

Traditional Vault

Work made by hand: pencil, ink, graphite and watercolour on paper. These drawings are the foundation of everything else in the archive, where line, gesture and observation come first.

Digital Vault

Illustration made on screen. The same line and the same ideas, now built from geometric forms, flat colour and light, and ready for print, screen and animation.

Cyborg Vault

Experiments in AI-assisted image making. Each piece starts from my own concept, references and art direction, testing how a personal visual language translates when a machine joins the process.
