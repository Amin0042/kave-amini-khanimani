Mission

I make images that move for a reason. Every project starts with one question: what should the viewer understand, or feel, in the first five seconds? Everything after that exists to answer it: the drawing, the type, the camera and the timing.

I draw first. My illustrations start as hand-drawn vector lines in Illustrator, so every asset I animate is my own, clean and ready to scale. Then I bring them into After Effects and give them depth, rhythm and a loop you can't see the seam in.

I build for the screen it lives on. I also develop web content in HTML, CSS and JavaScript, so I design motion with the final format in mind: file size, load time, mobile layouts and how an animation behaves inside a real page.

I bring a wider library. I grew up in Iran, spent time in India and now work in Canada. That shows up in my work as references people don't expect, like Indian miniature paintings turned into a seamless loop or Michelangelo's hands rebuilt in 3D, handled with respect for where they came from.

What I'm looking for: a studio, agency or in-house team where I can grow as a motion designer and illustrator, animating brand content, explainers and social campaigns, and helping bring them to the web. Freelance and contract projects are welcome too.
