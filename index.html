Kave Amini Khanimani

Motion graphic designer and illustrator in Ottawa. I draw every line by hand, then make it move: in After Effects, in 3D space, and on the web pages I build myself. I take images that generations before me made and kept, from Persian miniatures to Renaissance frescoes, and carry them into clean, modern motion that reads in seconds.

See The Work

Mission

Good motion serves one purpose: it makes an idea land faster than words can. I make illustrations and animations with a real idea at their heart, whether that’s a story, a piece of heritage entrusted to me, or a brand’s voice. And I build them to stand wherever they’re seen: on screen, on social and on the web.
